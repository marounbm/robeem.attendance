# Scenario attendance files

One Generic CSV file per employee, 2026-04-01 to 2026-05-30. There is an employee for every time schedule (and every team of it that works)
the template seeder makes, and the file of each holds every kind of day below, one after another on the days the schedule works, so a
single upload exercises everything. A schedule that works few days cannot fit all of them in one file (a weekend schedule works 17 days of
the period, each team of the alternate-weekend schedule 8 or 9), so those have two or three employees (`ALL1`, `ALL2`...) that together have every kind.
On a day the schedule does not work, the employee sometimes punches in and out anyway (`OFF_DAY`).

- `employees.csv` lists the employees. Load it on *System → Load test employees*: it makes them, puts each on its schedule and team and maps its number
  to device `SCN-1` (made if the tenant has none), so the files can be uploaded as they are (*Upload attendance*). Remove them again, with all their data,
  on *System → Delete test employees* with the same file. Nothing creates them at startup.
- `expected-results.csv` says, for every day, which kind of day it is and what the **starting rules** make of it: the exceptions, whether it waits
  for review or is approved, and the hours worked. Other rules give other answers.
- The employee number is the initials of the schedule (and of the team), a dash and `ALL`. The punches vary from day to day but are the same every run.
- These files are generated by `ScheduleScenarioTests`, which also checks what the engine makes of them against `expected-results.csv`'s rule.

| Kind of day | What happens |
|---|---|
| `PERFECT` | Checks in and out on time and takes the scheduled break: no exception, approved. |
| `LATE` | Checks in 11 to 45 minutes late and leaves on time: worked less than scheduled. |
| `EARLY_OUT` | Checks out 11 to 45 minutes early: worked less than scheduled. |
| `LATE_EARLY` | Both late and early out on the same day. |
| `OVERTIME` | Stays 15 to 90 minutes after the shift ends: overtime, approved unless pre-approval is required. |
| `NO_BREAK` | Punches in and out only: a break was scheduled and none was taken. |
| `LONG_BREAK` | The break runs 10 to 40 minutes over. |
| `LATE_BREAK` | The break starts 20 to 40 minutes after the scheduled start. |
| `EARLY_BREAK` | The break starts 20 to 40 minutes before the scheduled start. |
| `SHORT_BREAK` | A five-minute break-out and break-in (a toilet visit) on top of the normal day. |
| `MISSING_OUT` | A check-in and no check-out: a missing punch once the shift is over. |
| `MISSING_IN` | Break and check-out punches but no check-in: a missing punch. |
| `FIRST_IS_OUT` | A check-out typed where the check-in belongs, at the start, then the real check-out. |
| `LAST_IS_IN` | The real check-in, then a check-in typed where the check-out belongs, at the end. |
| `TAP_MORNING` | Two check-ins a few minutes apart at the start of the day. |
| `TAP_EVENING` | Two check-outs a few minutes apart at the end of the day. |
| `BREAK_IN_TWICE` | A break-in where the break-out belongs, then the real break-in. |
| `BREAK_OUT_TWICE` | The real break-out, then a break-out where the break-in belongs. |
| `NO_TYPES` | The clock sends no in/out type: the punches can only be read in order. |
| `IN_OUT_ONLY` | A clock with no break key: the break is a check-out and a check-in. |
| `ABSENT` | No punch on any scheduled day: a no-show. |
| `OFF_DAY` | Works the scheduled days, and punches in and out on days with no shift. |
| `H_PERFECT` | Paid by the hour: checks in and out, no shift to compare with. |
| `H_OVERNIGHT` | Paid by the hour: a session from the evening past midnight, counted on the day of the check-in. |
| `H_SPLIT` | Paid by the hour: leaves and comes back the same day. |
| `H_MISSING_OUT` | Paid by the hour: a check-in and no check-out. |
| `H_ABSENT` | Paid by the hour: no punch at all, and no exception for it. |

| Schedule | Team | Number prefix | Employees |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard Week | (everyone) | `SW` | 1 |
| Six-Day Workweek | (everyone) | `SDW` | 1 |
| Weekend Schedule | (everyone) | `WS` | 2 |
| Sunday-Thursday Workweek | (everyone) | `STW` | 1 |
| Alternate Weekend Schedule | Weekend A | `AWS-WA` | 3 |
| Alternate Weekend Schedule | Weekend B | `AWS-WB` | 3 |
| Four-Day Workweek | (everyone) | `FDW` | 1 |
| Continuous Operations (7-Day) | Morning Team | `CO7D-MT` | 1 |
| Continuous Operations (7-Day) | Afternoon Team | `CO7D-AT` | 1 |
| Continuous Operations (7-Day) | Night Team | `CO7D-NT` | 1 |
| Custom Workweek | (everyone) | `CW` | 1 |
| On Demand (Hourly Staff) | (everyone) | `ODHS` | 1 |
